CALC DUEL
1v1 Math Duel

What is this?

CALC DUEL is a two-player math duel on one phone. Both players see the same equation at the same instant. First correct answer takes the round — but a wrong guess freezes your side of the screen, so guessing costs you far more than it saves.

How to play

  • Rest the phone flat between two players, one on each end.
  • A question appears on both sides at once, with four possible answers — all very close to each other, so only the exact answer is safe.
  • Whoever taps the correct answer first wins that round's point.
  • The round ends the instant either player answers correctly — even if the other player is frozen. If both players get locked out, nobody scores.

The freeze penalty

  • A wrong answer freezes your side of the screen — no tapping until it thaws.
  • The freeze is about as long as that question normally takes to solve, so harder questions freeze you longer.
  • Answer wrong in the first quarter of that time and it counts as a guess: the freeze doubles.
  • You get two tries per round. A second wrong answer locks you out until the next round.

Game modes

  • Normal — friendly percentages (10%, 25%…) and one-step algebra, plus two easy warm-ups, then it ramps up.
  • Complex — awkward percentages (11%, 17%, 29%…), multi-step algebra with negatives, and multi-step expressions from round one.
  • Blackout — same as Complex, plus the equation vanishes mid-round so you have to solve from memory, and a wrong guess blanks out one or two digits on your side.

Scoring

  • Most correct answers across all rounds wins the match.
  • Questions get harder as the match goes on. Roughly 70% are percentages and algebra; the rest are classic arithmetic.
  • Reaction times are tracked quietly and revealed at the end, alongside the final score.
